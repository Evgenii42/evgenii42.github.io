EmTalk · Founder / Product Owner

From a shared idea to our own products.

EmTalk began with people who wanted to keep working together. For me, it became a way to connect research, product development and an environment where the team learns through practice.

Milestones and decisions

  • 2023 · The beginning

    Keep working together

    After my time in a startup incubator, team members suggested we keep going. We began developing our own idea, and I took on product work and organising the team.

  • Finding our format

    Choose a development path

    The project continued after the original team changed. We postponed the venture funding route while searching for PMF and began developing an environment for experiments, shared work and learning.

  • Early 2024 · Research

    Turn a broad vision into questions

    In our research workbook, we explored audiences, competitors and B2C/B2B hypotheses, separating features into prototype, MVP and later stages. This is a historical record, not a list of everything we shipped.

EmTalk · Founder / Product Owner

From a shared idea to our own products.

EmTalk began with people who wanted to keep working together. For me, it became a way to connect research, product development and an environment where the team learns through practice.

Milestones and decisions

  • 2023 · The beginning

    Keep working together

    After the startup incubator, we began our own project and built a team. Research from early 2024 helped us explore audiences and initial hypotheses; it remains a historical record.

  • Culture and project foundation

    Develop the team and formalise the project

    I adapted Spotify principles to a startup and contributors' personal goals. After preparing and revising the business plan, EmTalk joined Astana Hub and an LLP was established in Almaty. These details are based on my own account.

  • Ecosystem and priorities

    Separate the value and choose a manageable scope

    We moved from a super app to four directions. We reframed TAPL as EdTech and explored the concept with external users; it continues to develop gradually. The paused narrative stories and deeper gamification belong to a separate unnamed product unrelated to TAPL.

This is the part of the story I have shared so far. I am continuing to add specific episodes; further chapters are still to come.

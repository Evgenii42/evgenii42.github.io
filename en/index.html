What do you do when people are not using the product?+

At Samolet, I started with stakeholder conversations and workflow research. I identified barriers in user journeys and prioritised fixes. With the team, we made delivery faster, but product adoption remains low, so that work continues.

A CRM people rarely used: where I started changing things →
How do you make decisions when data is missing?+

Before we had a product analyst, I built a Python and Streamlit tool with AI assistance for dashboards and ClickHouse analysis. It gave us basic analytics. The analyst who later joined adopted the approach, and the tool remains in use.

No product analyst yet: building the tool myself →
How do you work with difficult stakeholders and vendors?+

In the WinLab assortment project, I temporarily paused direct discussions with the main stakeholder, heard other perspectives and involved leaders. In the checkout project, I challenged the vendor’s position through project management practices and set out my next steps. The outcomes were distinct: prepared requirements and priority with the vendor.

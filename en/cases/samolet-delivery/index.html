Start with the barriers users face

In April 2026, I joined a newly assembled team responsible for two CRM products: an agent workspace and a sales team lead panel. Users often chose other tools. I started with stakeholder introductions, customer discovery and workflow research. Conversations with sales managers and regional leads helped me identify barriers in the user flow and prioritise fixes.

Agree on the flow before development

The team's working process also needed attention. I developed an ICONIX-based approach to requirements using user flows, use cases and PlantUML sequence diagrams. We began reviewing scenarios with stakeholders before development and adjusting them early. QA joined earlier stages. Together, we introduced AI-assisted test case preparation with human review, automation and SDD.

What changed in delivery

Based on my figures, the team reduced time-to-market from 1.5 months to 2 weeks and defect leakage from approximately 30% to 11%. My contribution covered discovery and priorities, the requirements approach, early QA involvement and stakeholder work. This was a team outcome from changing our processes; I do not attribute it to a single tool.

The next challenge: product adoption

Product metrics have begun improving but remain weak. We continue investigating low usage, refining the backlog and improving workflows. Faster delivery supports this work, but does not by itself demonstrate product demand.

Business objective and expected impact

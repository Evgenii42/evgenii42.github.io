Statcontrol: when decisions depend on indispensable leaders

How I examined dependence on the founder and key managers, then designed an organisation that could distribute responsibility.

Organisation design

Prepared a diagnosis of constraints and a transformation proposal for the OptiRamp business.

Based on my 2022 HSE final project about Statcontrol. The evidenced stage is organisational diagnosis and change design.

How I approached it

  1. Symptoms and constraints
  2. Current Reality Tree and SWOT
  3. Future Reality Tree
  4. Roles and separate R&D / execution
  5. Transformation proposal

Strong specialists, limited autonomy

In my work on OptiRamp, communication between development and implementation depended on the founder, while operational questions converged on the programme manager. These were two levels of the same organisational challenge: delegation is difficult when decisions and communication depend on particular people.

Connecting the symptoms first

For my 2022 HSE final project, I analysed the situation using a Current Reality Tree and SWOT. I examined links between limited delegation, unclear task definitions, rework and unpredictable schedules. This was my diagnostic model, not a claim that every delay had one cause.

Designing a different way to work

In the Future Reality Tree, I connected proposed changes in responsibilities to the desired state. Project managers would coordinate engineering work, technical experts would support complex decisions, and the programme manager would have more room for strategic work. Direct knowledge exchange was intended to reduce dependence on repeated escalation.

Different processes for different uncertainty

I proposed a separate R&D group using Kanban for exploratory work. Better-defined engineering tasks would move into project execution with decomposition and a schedule. The purpose was to avoid promising predictable delivery before a technical solution existed, while keeping uncertainty from spreading across all work.

What I produced

The result was a transformation proposal for OptiRamp covering roles, collaboration, incentives and project economics. The document establishes the analysis and proposed solution. It does not establish that the whole organisation implemented the model or resolved founder dependence.

A model combining a shared product core, client customisation and gradual standardisation of development.

My account of the summer 2025 engagement. This case covers the proposal I prepared; approval, implementation and measured impact have not yet been established.

When every project starts from scratch

In summer 2025, I worked at NeuroCity as Head of the Project Management Office. I was invited to define a vision for moving towards a product approach and begin that transition. From what I observed, each project was built from scratch, deadlines were missed, and commitments around design concepts were not always backed by confidence in technical feasibility.

Propose a shared foundation for different clients

I prepared a consulting proposal: develop a shared product core in its own right and build client projects as customisations of it. This would allow common parts of a solution to be reused.

Separate core development from client project delivery

I proposed a Scrum team for core development, with Kanban and Waterfall for client projects. The model separated two types of work: developing the shared foundation and delivering specific projects. I proposed making the transition towards standardisation gradually.

What I prepared

I prepared a transition model: a shared foundation for client solutions, customisation and gradual standardisation. The central idea was that work on an individual project could contribute to a product useful to future clients too.

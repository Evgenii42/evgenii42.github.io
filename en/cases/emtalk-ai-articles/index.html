EmTalk: from sources to an article and a narration script

For wellbeing content, persuasive writing must not substitute for suitable evidence. Scientific context, SEO and editorial review have separate roles in our workflow.

How the material is prepared

  1. Article task
  2. Separate evidence and SEO context
  3. Draft generation
  4. AI editing and validation
  5. Markdown and narration script
  6. Handoff for further review

Different goals need different kinds of support

An article should answer a reader's question, be understandable and be discoverable through search. These goals can become confused: a search query may suggest a topic, but it does not substantiate a health claim. EmTalk's workflow assembles scientific publications, trial registry material and SEO information separately. This gives the data distinct roles before generation begins.

Do not stop at the first model response

One model produces the initial draft; another acts as an AI editor. It receives the draft and scientific context to review grounding, wording, safety and readability. Its instructions require unsupported claims to be removed or qualified. This adds an editing stage, rather than guaranteeing an error-free article.

Make part of the requirements testable in code

After AI review, code validates the response structure, title, minimum content length and editorial score thresholds. Safety has a higher threshold than the overall score. Review notes and reported changes are saved alongside the article. These numbers remain model-generated assessments, not independent expert appraisal.

Hand over an artifact the next person can work with

The output is Markdown marked reviewed-draft, with SEO fields and a link in the working spreadsheet. A separate branch adapts the article into a narration script. This is text for audio review, not a finished recording. The format keeps the material editable and separates preparation, review and subsequent use.

The boundary of this result

This case describes a configured content preparation workflow. An AI editor does not replace a person's responsibility for publication, especially in wellbeing topics. Configuration alone cannot show how many articles passed through the process, whether accuracy improved or how much editorial time was saved. Those outcomes need separate measurement.

A more flexible regional assortment

I worked at WinLab as a Senior Project Manager on an outstaff engagement. One project focused on a flexible assortment matrix: the company wanted to expand regional assortments using the potential of local producers. This was seen as an opportunity for revenue growth.

Broaden the conversation

Discussions with the main stakeholder were tense. At that stage, I temporarily paused direct communication with them and widened the group of participants. I spoke with other stakeholders to hear their perspectives and involved leadership.

Move towards requirements

Together with the team, we prepared business and functional requirements.

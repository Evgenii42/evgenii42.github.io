Technical change and contractor priority

Another WinLab project involved the checkout system and data transport: moving from a monolith to microservices and a Kafka message bus. Kristall was the contractor. As a Senior Project Manager on an outstaff engagement, one of my tasks was to secure priority with the contractor; meeting deadlines remained an important objective.

Discuss the project management approach

I discussed the contractor's position with their project managers, examined it through project management theory and practices, and set out my next steps. The discussion focused on how we would manage the project.

What changed after the conversation

Within a few hours, the contractor's representatives accepted my position and prioritised the project.

Decision story

EmTalk / Founder / Product Owner

Why we put venture funding on hold and focused on learning

While developing our first prototypes, we considered venture funding. The proposed terms made us assess the pace and working model we were ready to sustain.

  • StakeholdersStakeholders

Approach chosen

We put venture funding on hold while looking for product-market fit and chose a slower pace: experimenting, learning together and developing the team.

My account and retrospective interpretation as of October 2026. The project story is still unfolding.

New roles and first prototypes

After the team changed, we continued working. I invited my wife, who works in finance, a friend to take a management role and another friend to take the operations director role. We conducted analysis and customer discovery, found our first designer and began producing app prototypes. There was considerable uncertainty: we still needed to understand how to turn the concept into a sustainable product.

Assess the proposed path

We considered growing with venture funding. As I recall, we were offered approximately USD 100,000 for 30%. This was an offer, not funding we received. Under those conditions, I considered the amount too small relative to the stake and the commitments we would need to be ready for. We decided to put the venture route on hold while looking for product-market fit.

Make learning part of the work

We chose a different approach: collaboration, personal growth and learning together. We were prepared to move more slowly, explore different product approaches and gain experience. I wanted to build a team that could work through several changes to the concept and keep searching. The environment and community became part of the value we aimed to create.

How I see that choice now

Today, I describe this as building a team culture. To me, the way we worked together became our first product. As of October 2026, this structure has been operating for more than three years: we create products, experiment, make mistakes, lose some contributors and welcome others. For me, the value of this approach lies in being able to keep searching and learn through practice.

An international engineering environment

I joined Statistics & Control to work in an international environment with overseas clients. In a four-person team, I worked on Honeywell DCS reverse engineering and scripts for automating equipment-version migrations.

From technical work to coordination

I took on client communication, problem-solving and team coordination. Later, I moved to OptiRamp, digital twins, forecasting and modelling. I worked with Russian and international clients and formally moved into a project manager role.

How the work was organised

My responsibilities included requirements, risks, presales and delivery. I introduced Kanban, task evaluation, feedback and engineer onboarding processes, and prepared proposals for organisational change. I contributed to OptiRamp discovery, design, implementation and testing.

The connection to product work

This experience helps me understand complex workflows, collaborate with engineering teams and connect requirements to business needs.

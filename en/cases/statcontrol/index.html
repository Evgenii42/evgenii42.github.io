My work

Coordinated the team and delivery; introduced Kanban, task evaluation and engineer onboarding processes.

Based on my account, CV, portfolio and my 2022 HSE final project. Financial effects and payback figures in the project are assumption-based forecasts, not presented as realised outcomes.

An international engineering environment

I joined Statistics & Control to work in an international environment with overseas clients. In a four-person team, I worked on Honeywell DCS reverse engineering and scripts for automating equipment-version migrations.

Scale of responsibility

I managed projects with a combined value of approximately RUB 50 million per year. This estimate describes the scale of projects in my work, not revenue I personally generated or company profit.

From technical work to coordination

I took on client communication, problem-solving and team coordination. Later, I moved to OptiRamp, digital twins, forecasting and modelling. I worked with Russian and international clients and formally moved into a project manager role.

How the work was organised

My responsibilities included requirements, risks, presales and delivery. I introduced Kanban, task evaluation, feedback and engineer onboarding processes, and prepared proposals for organisational change. I contributed to OptiRamp discovery, design, implementation and testing.

Look at work as a system

In 2022, I focused my final project for the HSE Master in Management programme on organisational transformation at Statcontrol. The problem concerned predictable delivery and the use of engineering resources. I built current and future reality trees, carried out a SWOT analysis and proposed a model separating research work, project management and engineering execution.

Test the idea against costs and risks

For the proposed model, I calculated effort, project costs, investment needs, cash flows, NPV and payback. I also analysed risks and modelled schedule uncertainty using Monte Carlo simulation in Primavera Risk Analysis. Some inputs were estimates: this was a business case for a proposed change, rather than a report of realised savings.

From a recommendation to a testable proposal

The outcome was a change programme with a new role model, incentive proposals, a pilot plan and monitoring criteria. It demonstrates how I connect process diagnosis to organisational decisions, costs and risks.

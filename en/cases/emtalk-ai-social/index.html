One story across platforms, with publishing kept separate

A carousel, a short post and a LinkedIn article need different treatments. EmTalk has an active workflow definition for carousel preparation and a separate prototype exploring ideas and platform adaptation.

  • AI & automationAI & automation
Active carousel preparation and a separate prototype

Carousels receive a validated package of text and visual instructions. An inactive social prototype connects research, idea selection, candidate comparison and platform adaptation; its output requires review and is not a publication.

Based on inspection of n8n configuration on 10 October 2026. The carousel workflow is active; the social pilot and platform adapter are inactive and have no activeVersion. Readiness, executions and publications are unverified. Pilot budgeting uses an initial estimate and a check after calls, rather than a guaranteed spending cap before every call.

Inside the social prototype

  1. Research package and ideas
  2. Idea selection and three text candidates
  3. Two judges and ranking
  4. Adapt the shared narrative to platforms
  5. Check references against allowed claims
  6. NEEDS_REVIEW report, without publishing

Start with a specific format

In the active carousel workflow, the system takes source material and slide rows, then asks a model for text and visual instructions. Code checks required fields and alignment with source slides. Existing fields are preserved: generation fills gaps rather than unconditionally rewriting the entire package.

Move from format to a shared story

A separate social prototype takes a research package and idea portfolio through three candidates. Two model judges assess source fidelity, safety, brand voice, clarity and whether the central narrative is independent of the platform. Code ranks the results and marks close scores as requiring a human decision.

Adapt to the platform's context

The platform adapter receives a canonical narrative and a platform profile. It produces an appropriate treatment, while code checks the response structure and claim references: those must belong to the allowed set in the research package. The pilot includes branches for Telegram, Instagram and LinkedIn.

Keep a variant distinct from a publication

This social pilot remains an inactive prototype. It requires explicit consent for paid operations and ends with NEEDS_REVIEW and NOT_PUBLISHED. Adaptation can continue when selection is marked as requiring human input, so that label does not imply a stop at every step. The publishing decision remains outside this graph.

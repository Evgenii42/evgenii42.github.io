A system of causes behind missed deadlines

From July to November 2024, I worked as a Product Manager at Kickidler, an employee activity monitoring product. Untracked work, incomplete requirements, development tooling constraints and gaps in testing were interconnected. Planning needed to reflect how work actually moved from a task to a release.

Connect the problems

I prepared an assessment of the current state: a current reality tree and an analysis of project management, business and systems analysis, backend, frontend, DevOps and QA. I connected delivery problems to requirements, technical constraints and team collaboration. This produced a shared view of the changes needed across functions.

Make analysis part of the team’s work

I introduced a roadmap and used RACI and MoSCoW to structure ownership and priorities. I started establishing planning practices, prepared a template for analysts and helped develop requirements work. To assess progress, I proposed tracking delivery commitments, requirements errors and stakeholder feedback. Analysis was there to explain problems and guide the next decisions.

Turn the diagnosis into a change plan

In the target model, I connected the product lifecycle, documentation, testing and skills development. I prepared a phased plan with responsibilities, training and checkpoints. This is the consulting aspect of my product work: understand the current situation, propose a coherent model and describe the transition.

What changed during my engagement

Based on my assessment, time-to-market halved and product LTV increased by 20%. There were fewer bugs, while analytics, motivation and team eNPS improved. These were team results; I do not attribute the entire effect to one recommendation.

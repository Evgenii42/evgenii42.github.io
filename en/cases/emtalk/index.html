Research and released products

50+ customer discovery interviews. BRET and Decision Matrix published on Google Play.

Research and personal contribution are based on my materials. App publication and website content were checked against public pages on 9 October 2026. This is a project overview; the detailed founding story is still being developed.

Find useful scenarios

During research, I conducted more than 50 customer discovery interviews and explored the market and competitors. I assembled a part-time team without an initial budget. We were looking for useful ways to support well-being and decision-making.

My responsibilities

As founder and product lead, I worked on strategy, requirements, the backlog and priorities. I coordinated the team and prepared a pitch deck, investment materials and a GTM plan. The apps and website are a shared outcome of product, design and engineering work.

Four directions within one ecosystem

EmTalk is built around a sequence of user needs. First, find calm through breathing and other BRET practices. Then understand your current state through tests and articles. Next, structure a choice in Decision Matrix. Finally, move forward by developing skills and habits with Tapl. This connects Mental Health, Decision-making and EdTech in one product concept. The self-understanding direction is already available through tests and articles. Tapl has not been released, and a connected journey across all products remains a development direction.

What you can try

Together with the team, we brought BRET and Decision Matrix to Google Play. BRET brings together well-being practices. Decision Matrix helps compare options: its official listing describes categories, Pros & Cons, decision history and AI suggestions for alternatives. The EmTalk website offers five tests and nine articles, including an article with an audio version.

The current outcome

This overview covers the journey from research and team formation to published products. Public release does not establish product-market fit: revenue, retention and user impact data are not presented here.

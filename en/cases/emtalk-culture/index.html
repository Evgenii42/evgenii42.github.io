The EmTalk team: what helps people keep working together

What we built around the team

Our own approach to team collaboration and motivation; some contributors have continued working on EmTalk for two years or longer.

Based on my account. Contributor tenure and the current LLP and Astana Hub status are self-reported; registration records have not been independently checked. EmTalk's stated principles describe our approach rather than establish its effectiveness.

People bring their own goals

At this stage, the team worked without pay. I wanted to create an environment where contributing also helped people pursue their own goals: gaining experience, trying new roles and building products. I began treating motivation and collaboration as a product that also needed development.

Adapt principles to our team

I drew on Spotify principles and adapted them into our own approach to motivation and collaboration. On EmTalk's website, we describe the ideas that guide us: visible progress helps people move forward, responsibility starts with a small step, and every contributor has a goal of their own.

Keep working together over time

Some people have now been working with us for two years or longer. It matters to me that collaboration can continue for that long while the team and products evolve.

Give the project a formal foundation too

The next step was joining Astana Hub. We brought our analysis together, prepared a business plan and made minor revisions following feedback. The project became a participant, and an LLP was established in Almaty. As far as I know, our participation continues and the LLP remains operational.

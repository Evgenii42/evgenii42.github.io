How I approach architecture

Use models where variation is useful+

Options, drafts and creative proposals can have multiple valid answers. Code checks formats, links, states and constraints. The cases show this division in concrete workflows.

Quality requires different kinds of checks+

Valid JSON does not establish factual accuracy. A second LLM can support an editor, but its score does not replace source checking or educational expertise. Each artifact needs a clear account of what has been checked.

Make intermediate results visible+

Articles, scripts, course plans and individual frames are saved as separate artifacts. This makes the process easier to inspect and leaves room to revise a stage before moving on.

Technology does not establish product success+

A workflow alone does not prove demand or business impact. New tools still need checks of quality, cost and usefulness in practice. Released products need evidence from real user scenarios.
